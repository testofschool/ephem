## Scope that remains unsupported

- **Current-request plaintext confidentiality in an arbitrary useful worker:** intentionally disproved by the retained successful `ECHO CURRENT_REQUEST_CANARY` control (`tests/zero/test_root_manifest.py:175`; `evidence/zero/root-replay-v2.json:40-41,254-267`). The ciphertext relay avoids supplying plaintext to its worker; it does not make arbitrary plaintext computation opaque.
- **Semantic secret-free manifests:** `packaging/build-minimal-root.py:2-7,50-97` builds a new enumerated static-worker root and states that worker provenance must be reviewed. `root_manifest.rs:1-3` explicitly defines identity checking. An administrator approving a secret-bearing binary or manifest defeats the semantic claim without breaking a hash.
- **Transparent zero-cost integration for existing stateful applications:** the public static-worker/offline profile and opaque relay do not transparently cover databases, sessions, arbitrary durable writes or plaintext-dependent computation. No workload migration, customer integration cost or production performance study is supplied by these tests.
- **Physical unstealability or patch independence:** no evidence establishes containment after kernel/runtime/root-administrator/client compromise, hardware attacks, snapshots, physical memory acquisition or future cryptographic failure. Metadata, ciphertext and availability remain exposed. Buffer overwrites and no-swap/no-piped-core admission are bounded mechanisms, not proof of erasure. Existing threat boundaries remain at `docs/THREAT_MODEL.md:27-35,78-98`.
